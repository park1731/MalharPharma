Questions

What people ask us

Can’t find your answer? Call 93078 45862 or ask on WhatsApp.

Where is Malhar Pharma & Clinic?

We are at Shop No. 3, #199/3 & 203/4, Begur–Koppa Main Road, Koppa Village, Hulimangala Post, Jigani Hobli, Anekal Taluk, Bengaluru 560105. People come to us from Koppa, Hulimangala, Begur, Jigani, Anekal and Electronic City.

What are your timings?

The shop is open Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 10:30 PM, and closed on Sunday. The doctor sees patients in a morning session and an evening session, so please call 93078 45862 before coming for a consultation.

How do I book a doctor’s appointment?

Use the booking form on this page, which opens WhatsApp with your details ready to send, or call 93078 45862 or 94231 78631. You can also walk in during clinic hours.

Do you deliver medicines at home? Is delivery free?

Yes. We deliver medicines to homes in nearby areas, and delivery is free on orders above ₹650. Send your prescription or your list on WhatsApp to 94231 78631, or call us.

Do I need a prescription to order medicines?

Prescription medicines are given only against a valid prescription. Bring it to the counter or send a clear photo on WhatsApp. Over-the-counter products do not need one.

Which lab tests do you do, and when will I get the report?

We offer blood tests and other lab tests, ECG, and full-body health check-up packages. Most reports are ready within 24 to 48 hours.

What does a full-body check-up cost?

It depends on the tests in the package. We have packages for different ages and needs. Call or message us for the current packages and prices.

Does the doctor visit patients at home?

Yes, for patients who cannot come to the clinic. Call 93078 45862 to arrange a home visit.

Do you sell or rent medical equipment?

Yes. Medical equipment such as hospital beds and wheelchairs is available for sale and on rent. Call us to check what is available.

Do you have offers on medicines?

We run offers on medicines and health products from time to time. Ask at the counter or on WhatsApp for the current offers.

How can I pay?

You can pay by UPI, debit or credit card, or cash.

Is there parking?

Yes. There is parking for both two-wheelers and four-wheelers, and the shop is right on the main road.
